Eight ways to dress the celebration.

Each palette is shown on a mini version of the homepage: hero, marquee band and a light section with cards. Every one is checked against the logo's maroon, green, saffron and gold, since the logo isn't changing. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

How to choose

  • The logo decides more than taste does. It is maroon, green, saffron and gold. Palettes 1, 2 and 8 sit naturally next to it; 3, 4 and 5 will clash unless the logo goes on a cream or white plate.
  • For an audience of 16–35, "luxury" means restraint. One dark base, one jewel accent and gold, with cream for breathing room. A fourth strong colour starts to look like a wedding-card printer.
  • Images matter more than the palette. Whichever you pick, the food and event photos have to share its warmth, or the site will feel stitched together.
