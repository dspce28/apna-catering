Two directions. Pick one.

Each direction is a complete set of rules: two colours plus gold, two typefaces, one motif, one photo style and one way of moving. Whichever you choose, the whole site is rebuilt cleanly to those rules, with nothing borrowed from the other.

Direction A

Ivory Palace

A daytime palace wedding: ivory, soft light and space. Expensive because it is calm. The food and the photography do the talking, with maroon used only for words and buttons.

Palette

Maroon#3A0B14

Manrope: for everything you read.

  • MotifThe brochure roses, twice on the whole site, never more.
  • PhotosBright, warm, airy daylight. Arched frames for food.
  • MotionSlow fades and soft image wipes. Nothing spins or bounces.
  • Feels likeA five-star hotel brochure, or Sabyasachi's bridal pages.
  • RiskCan feel quiet; needs strong photos.

Direction B

Candlelit Durbar

A royal night reception: deep wine, candlelight and gold, with silver chafing dishes glowing in the dark. Dramatic and unmistakably big-fat-wedding. The brocade is the one motif, kept faint.

Palette

Night wine#1C0509
Candle ivory#F6EAD2

Typefaces

Playfair Display

Manrope: for everything you read.

  • MotifThe client's brocade, faint, on two sections only.
  • PhotosLow-light, candlelit, high contrast, golden highlights.
  • MotionCinematic: slow zooms, light sweeps, pinned scroll stages.
  • Feels likeA palace hotel at night, or a luxury restaurant launch.

How to choose

Show both to the client and ask one question: "Which one feels like your brand?" Whichever wins, the site is rebuilt cleanly to that one set of rules, with about seven homepage sections, keeping the scroll-wipe events stage, the fogged-glass venues and the menu board, all restyled.
