How it works

Four steps to a perfect day.

  1. Tell us your date

    Share your date, venue, guest numbers and traditions. We reply within one working day.

  2. Taste your menu

    Join us for a tasting with the chefs and try every dish before you decide.

  3. Meet your manager

    One event manager plans every ceremony with you, from the Roka to the reception.

  4. We serve the day

    Our own chefs and waiting team cook, serve and clear, so your family can celebrate.
