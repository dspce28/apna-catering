The wedding journey

Every ceremony, its own feast.

A Punjabi or Hindu wedding is five celebrations, not one. We plan a menu for each, so nothing repeats and nobody leaves hungry.

  1. 01The families meet

    Roka & Chunni

    Tea, pakoras, samosas and boxes of fresh mithai.

  2. 02The night before

    Mehndi & Jaggo

    Live chaat, tandoori grills and late-night chai for the dancers.

  3. 03The families embrace

    Milni

    Hot tea, pakoras and jalebi at the Gurdwara doors.

  4. 04The four laavan

    Anand Karaj

    A vegetarian Gurdwara breakfast and langar-style service, with no meat, eggs or alcohol.

  5. 05The party

    Reception

    The full Punjabi feast: makki di roti, sarson da saag, butter chicken and mixed grill.
