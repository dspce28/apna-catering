Need help?

Questions families ask us.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on 07818 478036 or send an enquiry. We reply within one working day.

How much should we budget per guest?

It depends on the menu, the number of events and the style of service. Tell us your date, guest numbers and traditions and we'll send a clear per-head quote, with no hidden extras for staff, china or equipment.

Do you cater at Gurdwaras?

Yes. For the Anand Karaj we serve a vegetarian breakfast and langar-style meal with no meat, eggs or alcohol, and we follow each Gurdwara's own rules on kitchen use and service.

Can you do pure vegetarian, satvik or Jain menus?

Yes. We cook fully vegetarian menus for Hindu ceremonies, and satvik and Jain menus with no onion or garlic, prepared separately in our kitchen.

Do you offer a tasting before we book?

Yes. Once you've shortlisted a menu, you're invited to a tasting with our chefs so the family can try every dish before deciding.

Is your meat halal?

Our meat is halal as standard, which is why many families choose us for Nikah and Walima receptions too. Tell us your requirements and we'll confirm them in writing.

Do you cater outdoors and outside Glasgow?

Yes. We cater marquees, gardens and country-house venues across Scotland, with our own field kitchens, live grills and full waiting team.
