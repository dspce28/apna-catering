Our heritage

A family kitchen that grew up with Glasgow's Asian community.

Since 1975 we've cooked for the biggest days in families' lives: weddings, walimas and reunions that bring three generations around one table. The recipes have been passed down. The standards haven't moved.

Every menu is built around your family's traditions, using fresh ingredients from suppliers we've trusted for years and served by a team that knows the difference between feeding guests and honouring them.

Anand Karaj & Reception

From the morning ceremony to the evening reception, we build a menu around your family's customs, including vegetarian-led menus for Gurdwara occasions and a full Punjabi spread for the party.

  • Punjabi classics: makki di roti, sarson, dal makhani
  • Live tandoor & mixed grill stations
  • Breakfast, ceremony & reception served by one team
