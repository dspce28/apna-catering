Our story · Love for food

A family kitchen that grew up with Glasgow's Asian community.

Welcome to Apna Catering, Glasgow's wedding and event caterers with a passion for fine food. Since 1975 we have cooked quality Indian and Asian dishes for the biggest days in families' lives, blessing weddings with an inventive menu of delights.

Our experienced team works with one mission: to uphold the values of every family we cook for. We use fresh ingredients from trusted suppliers, including small local family farms, and serve them with care your guests will notice.

  • Breakfast, ceremony & reception served by one team
