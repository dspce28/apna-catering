Who we are

A family kitchen that grew up with Glasgow’s Asian community.

Since 1975 we have cooked quality Indian and Punjabi dishes for the biggest days in families’ lives, blessing weddings with an inventive menu of delights.

Our team works with one mission: to uphold the values of every family we cook for. We use fresh ingredients from trusted suppliers, including small local family farms.
