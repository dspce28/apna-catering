Welcome to Apna Catering, Glasgow's wedding & event caterers with a passion for fine food. We specialise in quality Indian & Asian delicacies, blessing weddings with an inventive menu of delights. With an incredibly experienced team we uphold the values of our customers, creating meal plans of true prestige, and have built a reputation for excellence in hospitality.
We specialise in Sikh, Hindu and Muslim wedding catering. If you are inspired by authentic Indian food, allow Apna Catering to share that passion with you.
