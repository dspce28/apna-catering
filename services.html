Weddings

Sikh and Hindu weddings, done properly.

Anand Karaj & reception

From the Roka and Chunni to the Jaggo, the Milni and the Anand Karaj, then the reception that night. We plan every event and respect Gurdwara customs.

  • Vegetarian Gurdwara breakfast and langar-style service, with no meat, eggs or alcohol
  • Milni tea with pakoras, samosas and fresh mithai
  • Jaggo-night chaat and live tandoor stations
  • Full Punjabi reception: makki di roti, sarson da saag, butter chicken

Haldi, sangeet, pheras & reception

A Hindu wedding runs over several days, and every day deserves its own menu, with your family’s dietary customs at the centre.

  • Pure vegetarian, satvik and Jain menus, with no onion or garlic where needed
  • Haldi brunch: puri chole, kachori and fresh jalebi
  • Sangeet chaat stalls: gol gappe, papdi chaat, aloo tikki
  • Thali or buffet in Punjabi, Gujarati and South Indian styles
