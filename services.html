Weddings & events

Every family celebrates in its own way.

Anand Karaj & Reception

From the morning ceremony to the evening reception, we build a menu around your family's customs, including vegetarian-led menus for Gurdwara occasions and a full Punjabi spread for the party.

  • Punjabi classics: makki di roti, sarson, dal makhani
  • Live tandoor & mixed grill stations
  • Breakfast, ceremony & reception served by one team

Mehndi, Sangeet & Wedding

Multi-day celebrations call for menus that change with every event. We plan each one, from the mehndi chaat stall to the wedding feast, so every day feels distinct.

  • Fully vegetarian menus on request
  • Chaat stalls: gol gappe, papdi chaat, aloo tikki
  • Menus planned across every day of the celebration

Nikah, Mehndi & Walima

Rich, generous menus for the Nikah and Walima, with dietary and religious requirements agreed with your family at the first consultation and respected throughout.

  • Lamb rogan josh, biryani, seekh & chapli kebabs
  • Elegant plated or banquet-style service
  • Dessert buffets with rasmalai & kulfi
