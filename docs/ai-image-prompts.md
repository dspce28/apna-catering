# Apna Catering — AI image prompts (Gemini)

Demo images to replace the stock photos until real event photography is available.
Each prompt is mapped to the file it replaces. Generate, download at the highest
resolution Gemini offers, and send the files back with these names; they will be
converted to the 1200 / 2400 / 3840 px WebP sets the site uses.

## Style block — paste at the start of every prompt

> Photorealistic editorial photograph for a luxury Indian wedding caterer in Glasgow, UK.
> International, high-end event aesthetic: fine bone china, polished silver cutlery, crystal glassware,
> white linen, fresh flowers, warm candlelight and chandeliers. Colour mood: deep plum and aubergine
> accents with antique gold and ivory. Natural skin tones, real textures, shallow depth of field,
> shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm or 85mm lens. No steel plates or steel thalis, no plastic,
> no text, no logos, no watermarks, no distorted hands or faces.

## Checklist before you send them back

- [ ] Highest resolution available (aim for 2048 px or larger on the long edge)
- [ ] No text, signage or logos anywhere in the frame
- [ ] Hands, fingers and faces look natural (regenerate if not)
- [ ] Food looks real and appetising, not plastic or over-glossy
- [ ] File named exactly as below
